# PGS Oman — Complete Manager Virtual Assistant Dashboard

An AI-powered Virtual Assistant Dashboard designed specifically for the General Manager of a **Waste Management Contracting Company** (**PGS Oman**).

## Leftmost Navigation Sidebar — 2 Core Assistant Workspaces

### 1. Email Management Assistant
- **Automated Gmail Proposal Collection & Filtering**: Connects to the Manager's Gmail Inbox and collects **only** emails related to **Waste Management Proposals, RFPs, Quotations, Skip/Bin Inquiries, and Contract Documentation**. Unrelated promotional/spam emails are automatically placed in the **Filtered Out** bin.
- **PDF & Word (`.docx`) Contract Attachment Reading**: Automatically extracts text from attached RFPs, Bills of Quantities (BoQ), and contract schedules.
- **Plain-English AI Summary**: Summarizes client proposals in 3–4 bullet points and extracts key specs (Waste Streams, Locations, Bins/Skips Volume, Contract Duration, Deadline, and Estimated Monthly Value).
- **1-Click Action & Reply Drafter**:
  - **Accept & Send Quotation** (itemizes unit prices from your Rate Card)
  - **Request Site Inquiry / Visit** (proposes a technical site inspection)
  - **Ask for Missing Specs** (requests container counts & monthly tonnage)
  - **Approve Contract Terms** (confirms contract schedule, licensing & manifests)
- **1-Click Manager Approval**: Click **"Approve & Send Email to Recipient"** to immediately mail the reply back to the client, or save it to Gmail's `[Gmail]/Drafts` folder.

### 2. Social Media Marketing Assistant (LinkedIn & Instagram)
- **Connect & Manage LinkedIn + Instagram Accounts**: Configure your company's LinkedIn Page and Instagram Business account (`@username`).
- **Prompt + Multi-Photo Upload Studio**:
  - Give the AI a text prompt (or pick from 1-click waste management campaign ideas) and optionally upload **1 or more site/equipment photos**.
- **AI Marketing Image + Copy Generation**:
  - Automatically renders a **1080×1080 HD Branded Social Media Post Graphic** integrating your uploaded photos (or an AI industrial fleet illustration), company logo, category badge, headline, service highlights, and contact footer.
  - Generates tailored **LinkedIn B2B Post Copy** and **Instagram Feed Captions** with industry hashtags.
- **Live Side-by-Side Previews, Download & 1-Click Publish**:
  - Preview how the post looks on both **LinkedIn** and **Instagram**, download the HD PNG image, copy captions, or click **"Approve & Publish to LinkedIn + Instagram"**.
